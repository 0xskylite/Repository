- **XFADE (frames):** 7:8, 10:8, 12:6, 13:8, 14:8, 15:8. **FLASH_AT:** frame of line 2 (white, 4 f).
- **Kicks:** 3 floor beats (0.6/0.7/0.9), hammer landing (1.0), shot 9 +10 (0.5), shot 12 +20 (0.4), shot 16 +60 (0.5), last word (0.7).
- **Foreground:** cart (2b), lantern post (7b), bush (14c). All inside their own shot.
- **Rules kept:** no captions; DOF on ~1/3 of screen time; every hold creeps in; no static hold >= 4 f; rolls 4-5 deg; hard cuts only on big reframes.
- **Callbacks that carry the story:** hook = ending (shots 1 / 17), plumb bob swinging = plumb bob still (5 / 15), "Luckily / Unluckily" twin shot (8 / 9).
